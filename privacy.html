Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Suomi ("the App") is developed by 0Quantum ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the App.

Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. We operate no servers and no user accounts.

Data stored on your device

Your learning progress — words known, review schedules, streak, XP, accuracy and settings — is stored locally on your device in an on-device database. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not readable by us or by any third party;
  • is deleted permanently when you delete the App.

Third-party services

The App contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting and no social SDKs. Text-to-speech uses the speech engine built into your device (Apple), which processes the spoken word under Apple's own privacy policy. No text is sent to us.

Network access

The App works fully offline and does not require an internet connection to function.

Children

The App is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
